## 1. Multi-Agent Design Principles
- **Separation of Concerns**: Planner establishes hook strategy, Researcher gathers citations, Generator drafts post copy, and Reviewer acts strictly as an objective critic.
- **Architectural Guardrail**: The Reviewer agent outputs numerical scores, flaws, and actionable instructions, but is explicitly barred from generating replacement post copy directly.
- **Termination Invariants**: The loop terminates when either `overall_score >= quality_threshold` or `iteration >= max_iterations`.

## 2. 10-Dimension Editorial Rubric
1. `hook_impact`: Scroll-stopping power of the opening 2 lines.
2. `storytelling`: Narrative arc, human vulnerability, and pacing.
3. `professional_depth`: Technical accuracy and substantive insight.
4. `clarity`: Absence of buzzwords and fluff.
5. `engagement_potential`: Probability of debate and meaningful comments.
6. `originality`: Novel perspective vs regurgitated platitudes.
7. `structure`: Mobile-friendly line breaks and whitespace.
8. `actionability`: Concrete takeaways for the reader.
9. `emotional_resonance`: Empathy and inspiring energy.
10. `authenticity`: Genuine executive voice vs generic AI output.
